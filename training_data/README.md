# Drop training audio here

Drag audio files of dialogue into this folder. When you're done, tell me the
data is in and I'll pick the work back up.

**Any format works:** wav, mp3, m4a, flac, ogg, aac, or video files (mp4/mov/mkv,
I'll pull the audio out). Any length, and multiple speakers per file are fine.
I transcribe, split by speaker, remove silence and background noise, and
throw out unusable bits myself.

**What helps most**
- North Jersey / New York Italian-American men, the louder and more
  boastful the better
- Clean speech: little music, sound effects, or crosstalk under the voice
- Anything from a few minutes up to hours. More is better, and variety across
  speakers is fine
- Optional: a same-named `.txt` next to a file with its transcript, or a
  note in `notes.txt` about who/what is in which file

**Rights:** only add audio you have the right to use: your own recordings,
people who've agreed to it, a hired voice actor, public-domain or openly
licensed material. I can't train on commercial film or TV dialogue
(e.g. The Godfather or Goodfellas) without a license from the rights holders.
